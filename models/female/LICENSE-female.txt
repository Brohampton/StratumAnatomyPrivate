Female model files in this folder (*.bin.gz, manifest.json)

Derived from the Human Reference Atlas (HRA) united female reference model v1.5,
HuBMAP / CCF (https://humanatlas.io/), licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: pregnancy structures and schematic lymph-node interior removed; eyelid openings cut
in the skin; skin creases smoothed; organs and brain simplified; reclassified into layers and
regions; colours added; packed into compressed binary buffers for web display.
