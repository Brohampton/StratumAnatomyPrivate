3D model files in this folder (*.bin.gz, manifest.json)

Derived from:
- Z-Anatomy, the libre 3D atlas of anatomy (https://www.z-anatomy.com/), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0,
  itself built on BodyParts3D.
- BodyParts3D 4.0, (c) The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), licensed CC BY 4.0
  (https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/). Skin, hair and lips taken from here
  and warped onto the Z-Anatomy skeleton.

Changes: geometry simplified, re-oriented, reclassified into layers and regions, colours added,
packed into compressed binary buffers for web display.
